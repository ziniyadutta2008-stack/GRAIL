# GRAIL homepage video generation brief

Use this brief outside Cursor to produce the cinematic hero assets. The application loads them from `frontend/public` and remains fully usable with the poster fallback until they are present.

## Exact deliverable paths

Place finished files here:

- `frontend/public/videos/grail-governance-hero.webm`
- `frontend/public/videos/grail-governance-hero.mp4`
- `frontend/public/videos/grail-governance-mobile.webm`
- `frontend/public/videos/grail-governance-mobile.mp4`
- `frontend/public/images/grail-governance-hero-poster.webp` (optional replacement for the SVG poster)

## Technical targets

- Duration: 8–12 second seamless silent loop
- Desktop file size: approximately 4–5 MB or less
- Mobile file size: approximately 2–3 MB or less
- Frame rate: 24 fps is sufficient
- No audio track
- Seamless first-to-last-frame transition
- Abstract, unreadable UI graphics rather than fake legible dashboards

## Creative direction (exact)

The finished footage should be an 8–12 second seamless silent loop containing approximately five connected scenes:

1. An AI governance lead reviewing a new AI-system intake and risk classification.
2. A data steward and engineer examining data lineage, consent, quality and sensitivity.
3. A model developer and independent validator reviewing performance, fairness and testing evidence.
4. Legal, privacy, security and model-risk colleagues discussing controls and remediation.
5. An executive governance council reviewing evidence and making a controlled deployment decision.
6. A monitoring scene showing production assurance, incidents and lifecycle review.

Creative requirements:

- Diverse professionals across gender, age and ethnicity
- Realistic enterprise working environment
- Calm, confident and collaborative behaviour
- Authentic review and challenge rather than staged handshakes
- Subtle camera movement
- Dark navy, slate, teal and restrained cyan colour grading
- Natural lighting and credible office environments
- Abstract, unreadable UI graphics rather than fake legible dashboards
- No real company brands or logos
- No real patients, applicants or customers
- No sensitive records or identifiable personal data
- No biometric-surveillance aesthetic
- No robots, holographic brains or science-fiction imagery
- No neon-purple “generic AI” appearance
- No visible text generated inside the footage
- No resemblance to identifiable public figures
- No face cloning
- No audio
- Seamless first-to-last-frame transition

## Product context

GRAIL is Governance, Risk, Assurance and AI Lifecycle — an Enterprise AI and Model Risk Governance Control Plane. Footage should feel credible for a global bank, regulator, consultancy or enterprise risk platform.
